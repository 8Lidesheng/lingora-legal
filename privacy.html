中文版

Lingora（以下简称“我们”）非常重视你的隐私。本政策说明我们如何收集、使用、存储和保护你的信息。

一、我们收集的信息

  1. 你主动提供的信息：你设置的昵称、选择的学习语言。
  2. 学习与使用数据：学习进度、经验值、连续学习天数。上述数据仅存储在你的设备本地（iOS UserDefaults 独立存储空间），用于为你提供学习记录功能。
  3. 购买信息：当你通过 Apple App Store 进行内购时，付款流程由 Apple 完成，我们不会接触或存储你的银行卡、支付密码等支付信息。交易状态通过 Apple StoreKit 框架进行校验，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。
  4. 语音功能：本应用使用 iOS 系统语音合成朗读单词与句子，朗读在设备本地完成。在口语跟读练习中，我们会请求麦克风权限以录制你的发音，用于本地发音对比与练习反馈；录音仅在设备本地处理，不会上传到我们的服务器。拒绝授权后仍可正常使用文字答题等全部学习功能。

二、设备权限

  1. 本应用仅在口语跟读练习时请求麦克风权限，不请求通讯录、相册、相机、定位等其他隐私权限。
  2. 本应用不包含广告标识符（IDFA）相关的追踪功能，不进行跨应用追踪。

三、第三方服务

  1. Apple StoreKit：用于完成应用内购买与订阅，相关信息请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。
  2. 本应用不接入第三方广告、统计或数据分析 SDK。

四、信息的存储与删除

  1. 你的全部个人数据均存储在本机。卸载本应用或使用“注销账号并删除数据”功能，相关本地数据将被删除。
  2. 注销账号路径：我的 - 注销账号并删除数据。注销后学习进度将无法恢复。
  3. App Store 订阅关系由 Apple 管理，如需取消请在系统设置中操作。

五、未成年人保护

  1. 本应用提供青少年模式，开启后将禁用应用内购买。
  2. 我们不会主动收集未成年人的个人敏感信息。监护人如发现未成年人在未经允许的情况下进行了消费，可通过 Apple 官方渠道申请退款。

六、信息安全

我们采用合理的技术与管理措施保护你的信息安全。本应用不包含追踪域名，未接入第三方统计服务，数据不外传。

七、你的权利

你有权访问、更正、删除你的个人信息。由于数据存储于本机，你可通过注销账号或卸载应用实现删除。

八、政策更新

我们可能适时更新本政策，更新后将在应用内公示。

九、联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问、意见或建议，可通过邮箱 rudykobe@outlook.com 与我们联系。

English Version

At Lingora we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use, store, and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: the display name you set and the learning language you choose.
  • Learning and usage data: progress, XP, and streak. These are stored only locally on your device to provide learning-record features.
  • Purchase information: in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive or store your card number or payment password; transaction status is verified through Apple’s StoreKit framework and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Voice features: the app uses iOS on-device text-to-speech to read words and sentences. During speaking practice, microphone access is requested to record your pronunciation for on-device comparison and feedback. Recordings are processed only on your device and are never uploaded. If you decline microphone access, you can still use all text-based learning features.

2. Device Permissions

The app requests microphone access only during speaking practice. It does not request contacts, photo library, camera, or location permissions. The app does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and performs no cross-app tracking.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit: used to complete in-app purchases and subscriptions. See Apple’s privacy policy for details.
  • The app does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK.

4. Storage and Deletion

All your personal data is stored on your device. Uninstalling the app or using “Delete Account & Data” removes the relevant local data. Deletion path: Profile → Delete Account & Data. After deletion, progress cannot be recovered. App Store subscriptions are managed by Apple; cancel them in your system Settings.

5. Minors

The app offers a Youth Mode that disables in-app purchases. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information from minors. If a guardian discovers an unauthorized purchase by a minor, a refund may be requested through Apple’s official channels.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. The app contains no tracking domains and no third-party analytics services; data is not transmitted off your device.

7. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal information. Because data is stored locally, you can delete it by removing the account or uninstalling the app.

8. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted within the app.

9. Contact

For questions or comments, contact us at rudykobe@outlook.com.
